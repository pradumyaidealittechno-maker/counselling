What is Job DNA™?
Job DNA™ is Intelligens’ proprietary role-intelligence framework that transforms a job description into a structured, human-approved model used to generate high-quality interview questions and explainable hiring recommendations.
Unlike traditional systems that rely on static job descriptions or generic interview templates, Intelligens trains AI interview agents on Job DNA™, ensuring interviews are role-aware, consistent, and fair.
________________


Why Job DNA™ Exists
Hiring today is broken because:
* Job descriptions are unstructured text

* Interview questions are often generic

* Evaluations are subjective and inconsistent

* Hiring decisions are difficult to explain or audit

Job DNA™ solves this by introducing a single source of truth that aligns resume screening, interviews, evaluation, and hiring decisions.
________________


Core Principle
AI interview agents in Intelligens are trained on Job DNA™, not on static job descriptions.
This is why interview questions generated by Intelligens are more relevant, more consistent, and higher quality.
________________


How Job DNA™ Works
   1. A job description is uploaded or imported (e.g., LinkedIn)

   2. AI generates a structured Job DNA™ draft

   3. HR reviews, edits, and approves Job DNA™

   4. AI interview agents are trained on the approved Job DNA™

   5. Interviews, evaluation, and recommendations reference the same Job DNA™

No AI training or interview generation occurs without human approval.
________________


Job DNA™ Structure
Job DNA™ is multi-dimensional and explainable.
Skill DNA
Defines the technical and functional abilities required for the role.
      * Must-have skills

      * Nice-to-have skills

      * Depth of expertise

      * Tools and technologies

________________


Experience DNA
Defines the level and type of experience expected.
         * Seniority level

         * Years of experience (flexible)

         * Domain exposure

         * Complexity of problems handled

         * Decision-making responsibility

________________


Behavioral DNA
Defines how the candidate is expected to behave at work.
            * Ownership

            * Accountability

            * Collaboration

            * Adaptability

            * Leadership traits

________________


Communication DNA
Defines how the candidate should communicate.
               * Clarity of thought

               * Structured explanations

               * Confidence and articulation

               * Stakeholder communication ability

________________


Cultural DNA (Optional)
Defines environment fit, not personality.
                  * Startup vs enterprise pace

                  * Autonomy vs process orientation

                  * Speed vs precision

This dimension is optional and always transparent.
________________


Importance Weights
Each Job DNA™ trait is assigned an importance weight:
                     * Low

                     * Medium

                     * High

These weights guide:
                        * Interview question focus

                        * Evaluation emphasis

                        * Hiring recommendation logic

________________


Job DNA™ and Interview Questions
Interview questions in Intelligens are derived from Job DNA™, not from raw job descriptions.
Each interview question is:
                           * Mapped to one or more Job DNA™ traits

                           * Tagged with importance weight

                           * Designed to elicit specific signals

Example:
“This question evaluates System Design (Skill DNA – High)”
________________


Job DNA™ and Candidate Evaluation
Candidate responses are evaluated against Job DNA™, ensuring:
                              * Consistency across candidates

                              * Reduced interviewer bias

                              * Explainable scoring

Evaluations are broken down by Job DNA™ dimensions rather than a single opaque score.
________________


Explainable Hiring Recommendations
Job DNA™ enables clear, human-readable recommendations such as:
“Recommended to Hire due to strong alignment with Skill DNA and Experience DNA, with minor gaps in Communication DNA.”
This supports auditability, trust, and compliance.
________________


Human-in-the-Loop Governance
Job DNA™ is AI-assisted but human-governed:
                                 * AI proposes Job DNA™

                                 * Humans review and approve

                                 * Changes never happen silently

                                 * Approval is mandatory before AI training

Humans always remain in control.
